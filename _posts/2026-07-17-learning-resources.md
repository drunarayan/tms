All resources you will need - physical, electronic or otherwise are listed below.

## Physical Resources
1. Your laptop with a camera and microphone so that you can partcipate in web meetings. You will also need the Chrome Browser which we will use primarily in the virtual classroom
1. You will need a calculator, separate from your phone or laptop for quick calculations and estimates. The calculator on the laptop or the phone are not really recommended.
1. You will need a Geometry set with a compass, rulers, and protractor. You will need multiple HB or darker pencils for the compass and to draw lines.
1. You will need a bound notebook, especially for the meenakshi school lessons. I'll be asking you to make notes, write down values, numbers, references and sketches on the bound notebook quite often.  Please come prepared. Taking notes on the laptop for such things is not practical.

## Website Resources
1. [The Meenakshi School (TMS) Website](https://drunarayan.github.io/tms/)
1. [Google Meet Link](https://meet.google.com/hwo-ttfv-smv). Meetings are usually held on Saturday Mornings at 8:00 AM India Time.
