In this lesson (which will last multiple weeks), we will create a Github site like real research students as a web repository of all of our academic and personal accomplishments. Specifically, we will include our research projects, publications, scientific, professional, and personal interests.  Everything we store will be published as a website for the general public to see from anywhere in the world and get to know you as a professional. You will then officially join the community of millions researchers worldwide.

We will create the default website first and methodically update it with your own information vis The Meenakshi School lessons. Who knows, your patti Meenakshi herself might see it as she watches over all of us attending her school!
