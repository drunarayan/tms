Dear family! I'm Chandru. I’m a "retired" engineer/astronomer at heart turned teacher. Ever since I first took my first physics class in high school and learned to know the night sky, I have pursued engineering, mathematics, astronomy as a passion, and as a career. I am a devoted bicyclist and a "pretend" runner who loves to travel around the world to practice my hobbies. 

The picture above shows our matriarch for The Meenakshi School, my mom holding me when I was just a 1 year old (64 years ago).  It is in her memory that we have established our school for all motivated students of science, nature, art and music.

Check out [my personal website](https://www.chandrunarayan.com/) for more information on my other projects.
